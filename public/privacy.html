Privacy Policy

Short and honest, the way we run everything.

What we collect

This site has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking pixels. The only information we collect is what you type into the contact form — your name, phone (if you give it), email, and message — and we use it only to answer you and to send opening updates if you ask for them.

The map

The visit page loads a Google Maps embed. Google may set its own cookies once the map loads; that happens between you and Google, under Google's policy. Every other page on this site loads nothing from anyone else.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Railway (railway.app), which sees standard server logs (IP address, browser, page requested) as part of keeping the site online.

Your choices

Email Thehoneybistro@hotmail.com to ask what we've got from you, or to have your message deleted. We'll do it — no forms, no runaround. This policy covers this site only.
