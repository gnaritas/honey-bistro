Questions people ask us

Before opening day

When are you opening?

We're aiming for January 2027 — as early as mid-January, if everything goes as planned. The best way to hear it first is to send us a note and follow the Facebook page.

Where exactly is it?

501 Commerce Drive in Savoy — the space that housed Kyle's Corner, right beside El Rancho Grande II, minutes from Champaign and Urbana. See the visit page for the map.

What's on the menu?

Favorites from Star 57's menu — eggs, pancakes, bacon, waffles, and more — plus new exclusive plates and signature honey dishes. Details are on the menu page.

What are the hours?

Planned daily, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. — breakfast and lunch only. The tighter schedule lets Endrit and Nita keep cooking and serving at Star 57 Diner in Tuscola too.

Will Star 57 Diner stay open?

Yes. Star 57 Diner at 1104 Tuscola Blvd stays open — breakfast served all day, seven days a week. Savoy just gets the second chapter.
