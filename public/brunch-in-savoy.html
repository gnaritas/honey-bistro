Brunch is moving to Savoy

No more Saturday drives to Tuscola for the pancakes. The Honey Bistro opens on Commerce Drive in Savoy in January 2027 — Star 57's cooking, breakfast-and-lunch hours, and honey where honey belongs.

What brunch looks like here

Brunch, the honest diner way: eggs any style, stacks of pancakes, crisp bacon, golden waffles — plus new plates you can't get at the diner, and signature honey dishes that turn a breakfast into a reason to come back.

Planned hours are daily, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. — early enough for the pre-work egg sandwich, late enough for the post-church waffle. We close at three on purpose: the owners run Star 57 Diner in Tuscola too, and both kitchens deserve full attention.

Why Savoy

“It's a bigger town, and we have so many people already that are supporting the diner from Savoy,” Nita Zekjiri told The News-Gazette. “So we just wanted to bring something there as well.” The answer is a second restaurant, placed right where those customers already are.

The space

The Honey Bistro takes over the storefront at 501 Commerce Drive that housed Kyle's Corner for nine months — and the Industrial Donut for the six years before that — right beside El Rancho Grande II, on the Commerce Drive strip minutes from Champaign and Urbana.
