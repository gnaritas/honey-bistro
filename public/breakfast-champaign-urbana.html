Real breakfast, minutes off the 26

For gamedays, move-in weekends, and every ordinary Saturday — The Honey Bistro puts diner-quality breakfast a short drive from Champaign and Urbana, on Savoy's Commerce Drive strip, opening January 2027.

Right in the middle of everything

Savoy sits between Champaign and Urbana, and 501 Commerce Drive sits on the busiest stretch of it — beside El Rancho Grande II, easy from US-150, I-74 and I-57 without ever aiming at downtown traffic. From Champaign or Urbana it's a short, boring, easy drive. That's the point.

The track record behind it

This isn't a first rodeo. Endrit and Nita Zekjiri opened Star 57 Diner in Tuscola in 2025 — took over the old diner, cleaned up the menu, and built a line of regulars who keep it busy seven days a week. Nita learned breakfast at Lakeview Family Restaurant in Shelbyville long before that. Savoy is the second restaurant, not the practice run.

What to expect on opening morning

Eggs any style, pancakes, bacon, waffles, and more favorites from the Tuscola menu — plus new plates built for the Savoy kitchen and signature honey dishes. Planned daily hours of 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. mean a warm griddle from early coffee to a late lunch.
